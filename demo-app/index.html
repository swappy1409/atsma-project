What the ranking says

Citation identifiers (ISBN, DOI, ISSN) sit at the top: nearly every referenced article links to them. International Organization for Standardization has only 428 in-links yet ranks #16, because its links come from those top identifier pages. PageRank weighs who links to you, not just how many.
